## Design Context

### Users
Players of Legends FC, an amateur Fútbol 7 team (Barcelona area, Spanish/Catalan). They open the link on their phones right after a match, often at night, to rate teammates and vote an MVP. One admin manages players, matches and the calendar, and shows off the FIFA-style cards.

### Brand Personality
Matchday, proud, a bit cheeky. It should feel like the club's own matchday programme and stadium scoreboard, not a SaaS form. Emotional goal: "our team is legit".

### Aesthetic Direction
- Dark, warm black and gold taken from the crest (gold lion on black). Neutrals tinted toward gold (hue ~85).
- Display: Big Shoulders Display (stadium signage, scoreboards, numbers). Body: Archivo (with width axis; condensed for labels).
- Subtle pitch-line motifs (center circle, halfway line). No neon, no purple/cyan, no glassmorphism.
- The cards are the hero; MVP card is black-and-gold foil.

### Design Principles
1. Phone first: thumb-reachable bottom tab bar, 44px+ touch targets, no sideways scroll.
2. Rating must be fast: compact grid, values color-coded by card tier, visible progress.
3. Gold is rare: used for the current tab, primary actions and highlights only.
4. Numbers are display type: scores, ratings and stats use the scoreboard face.
